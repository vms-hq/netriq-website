what it does

Capture, understand, act — on the same feed.

A VMS records. Netriq records and watches. Live and after the fact.

01 / CAPTURE

Bring every camera into one view.

Auto-discovery scans your LAN, reads codecs, registers cameras in bulk. SIM and Wi-Fi cameras stream over a relay so you don't need a public IP at every site.

  • RTSP, ONVIF, Hikvision, Dahua, Frigate
  • WebRTC live view, sub-second latency
  • H.265 and H.264, automatic codec fallback
  • Continuous recording with retention controls
02 / UNDERSTAND

The AI that watches the wall.

Real-time object detection, license-plate recognition, and cross-camera person/object tracking. The system filters noise so you only see what matters.

  • Person, vehicle, and object detection
  • License-plate recognition (HSRP-tuned)
  • Persons of Interest, tracked across cameras
  • Polygon zones, counting, occupancy
03 / ACT

From signal to response.

Alerts route to WhatsApp or email. Forensic scan replays archived footage with the same AI. Vehicle registry and gate log give you a queryable record, not a tape to scrub.

  • WhatsApp and email alerting
  • Forensic scan over recorded footage
  • Gate log and vehicle registry
  • Searchable detection history
