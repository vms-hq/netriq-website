Gated communities

The CCTV is installed. The system isn’t actually working.

Most societies have 30 to 200 cameras spread across vendors, gates, and DVR generations. Guards watch a wall of feeds and miss what matters. Footage is reviewed only after an incident.

What Netriq does at a society

  • Single console across all gates and all camera vendors
  • Plate-driven gate log: residents, visitors, unknown vehicles, blacklist
  • After-hours person and vehicle alerts on perimeter zones
  • Visitor flow tracking from gate to lobby
  • WhatsApp alerts to RWA / facility heads on configured rules

Pain points it removes

  • 16-screen video walls nobody watches
  • Mixed vendors that no single VMS supports cleanly
  • Paper visitor registers at gates
  • Camera offline for days before someone notices
  • Hours of scrubbing tape after every incident
Hospitals

Coverage where attention matters most.

Lobbies, vehicle gates, restricted zones, store rooms, parking. Different teams need different visibility — security, facilities, administration. Netriq carves the same camera set into role-scoped views.

Use cases

  • Vehicle gate: ambulance / staff / visitor flow with plate logs
  • Restricted-zone alerts (after-hours entries, unknown personnel)
  • Lobby and corridor occupancy and dwell tracking
  • Forensic scan to retrieve footage for incident reviews
  • Site-by-site multi-hospital control under one console

Why it works for healthcare

  • Vendor-agnostic — we work with the cameras already installed
  • Role-based access keeps clinical and ops teams out of each other’s views
  • Audit log of every action on the platform
  • Hybrid storage — recordings can stay on-prem if compliance requires
Schools & campuses

From entry gate to back lane — one platform, one record.

Schools run a wide camera footprint with limited security staff. Most footage gets reviewed only after a complaint. Netriq does the watching so staff can do the responding.

Use cases

  • Gate entry: plate logs of pickup vehicles, drivers, visitor cars
  • Perimeter alerts during after-hours and weekends
  • Restricted zones (labs, store rooms, server rooms)
  • Crowd density and dwell at choke points
  • Forensic scan over recorded footage for incident review

Operational fit

  • Works across the multi-vintage camera mix typical of campuses
  • Cloud remote access — principal / management can view from anywhere
  • WhatsApp / email routing for fast notification
  • Multi-site for school chains across cities

Use cases
