02 — ARGUMENTS

What we actually mean by “the intelligent eye.”

Vendor-agnostic, network-agnostic

Most premises run a multi-vintage mix — Hikvision DVRs that pre-date the manager, Dahua cameras that arrived with the new wing, a couple of 4G SIM cams covering the back gate, a Wi-Fi cam in the manager’s cabin. NetrIQ absorbs all of it. RTSP, ONVIF, HTTP snapshot fallback. Public IP or no public IP. The platform doesn’t care.

Hybrid edge + cloud, by design

Real-time detection runs at the edge so alerts fire in the second they should. Recording stays local for bandwidth and retention. The cloud handles remote viewing, multi-site, and the cross-camera intelligence that benefits from a global view. Pick what lives where; the platform doesn’t force you.

Software-only and open

NetrIQ is software. We don’t sell you a camera. That means you keep your hardware leverage, you keep choice, and you keep portability. For regulated markets, pair NetrIQ with NDAA-compliant cameras. For everyone else, run it on the Hikvision, Dahua, CP Plus, and Sparsh hardware your sites already standardised on.

Hardened for the field, not the lab

Real deployments run mixed networks, mixed vintages, intermittent uplinks, and plate formats that defeat off-the-shelf OCR. NetrIQ’s plate engine handles HSRP and regional formats with multi-frame consensus. The product is tested against the conditions that actually trip surveillance — low light, vendor-mix, network drops — not the showroom.

03 — PRICING

Pay for what you turn on, per camera.

A car-park gate camera doesn’t need the same tier as the control-room live wall. NetrIQ prices by camera tier, not by camera count alone.

Record & detect

Continuous recording plus baseline AI detection. Alerts on configured rules. Right tier for most cameras on a site.

Plates & gates

License-plate recognition added on top of record + detect. Auto-populated gate log and vehicle registry.

Analytics & PoI

Counting, occupancy, heatmaps, and Persons-of-Interest tracking. For control-room and high-value coverage.

Live wall

Cameras intended for the operations video wall. Full live-view, no analytics requirement.

Forensic-on-demand

Lower-cost tier for cameras you mostly archive but occasionally scan. Forensic detection runs only when you ask.

Custom

Multi-site, multi-tenant, regulated environments. We’ll size and price it on the actual deployment.
