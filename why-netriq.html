FAQ

Common questions

Does NetrIQ work with my existing CCTV cameras?

Yes. NetrIQ is software-only and vendor-agnostic: it connects to Hikvision, Dahua, CP Plus and Sparsh natively, ingests Frigate NVRs, and discovers any generic ONVIF camera — plus Wi-Fi and 4G SIM cameras. We don't sell cameras; the hardware you already paid for stays where it is.

Do I need a public IP or port forwarding to view cameras remotely?

No. For remote sites the Site Agent bridges your cameras over a cloud relay, so live view and alerts work with no public IP, no port forwarding and no firewall holes.

Can NetrIQ read Indian HSRP number plates?

Yes. License-plate recognition is tuned for HSRP plates and uses multi-frame consensus OCR, tested against low light, mixed-vintage cameras and network drops — not lab conditions.

Is footage stored locally or in the cloud?

Both — NetrIQ is hybrid by design. Recording can stay local while the cloud handles remote view and cross-camera intelligence; you choose the mix per site.

How is NetrIQ priced?

By camera tier, not by count alone. You pay for the analytics you turn on per camera — from a low-cost forensic-on-demand tier up to plates, analytics and full live-wall tiers. Contact us for a quote sized to your deployment.

What's the difference between NetrIQ VMS and NetrIQ Suite?

NetrIQ VMS is the video-management foundation: ingestion, live view, recording, event-anchor playback and basic detection. NetrIQ Suite adds the full AI surface — visual search, person-of-interest Re-ID, tailgating, PTZ-360, zone rules, LPR, gate log, multi-channel alerts and forensic export with chain-of-custody. Verticals are presets on top of either edition, not separate products.
