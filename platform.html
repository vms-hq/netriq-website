01 — CAPTURE

Bring every camera into one view.

Camera-agnostic by design — we connect to whatever you have, over the network you have, and present it as one system.

  • Multi-vendor ingestion — Hikvision, Dahua, CP Plus, Sparsh, Frigate NVRs, any ONVIF / RTSP camera
  • Auto-discovery — finds cameras on the LAN and onboards them in bulk
  • Live view — WebRTC, sub-second latency, H.265 / H.264 with automatic fallback
  • Continuous recording — configurable retention, every detection linked to the exact frame
  • Event-anchored playback — click an alert, the timeline opens on the moment, no scrubbing
  • PTZ & 360° — pan/tilt/zoom and fisheye dewarp from the live grid
02 — UNDERSTAND

The AI that watches the wall.

A generic VMS detects motion. NetrIQ detects what is moving, identifies it across cameras, and surfaces only what matters.

  • Object detection — person, vehicle, animal and common classes, in real time
  • License plate recognition — HSRP-tuned; auto-populates the gate log and vehicle registry
  • Persons of Interest — mark once, tracked across every camera by cross-camera re-identification
  • Forensic scan — run the same AI over recorded footage for any time range and cameras
  • Zone rules — unmanned-gate, occupancy, intrusion and tailgating, firing only in context
  • Natural-language search — type a plain-English description, get back matching clips
03 — ACT

From signal to response.

Detection is only useful if it reaches the right person at the right moment — or answers the right query after the fact.

  • Multi-channel notify — in-app, email and WhatsApp from one engine, with audience routing and escalation
  • Gate log & vehicle registry — every entry and exit captured automatically, searchable and exportable
  • Detection history — every event with thumbnail, class, confidence, camera, time and a link to the frame
  • Forensic export — evidence packs with hashed clips and signed manifests for chain-of-custody
  • Roles & multi-site — one console across sites, permissions enforced at the API
  • Event de-duplication — duplicate alerts filtered so the inbox stays signal

EDITIONS

Two editions. Every vertical runs on them.

Pick NetrIQ VMS or NetrIQ Suite per site. The verticals are presets on top of a tier — not separate products. Move up later without re-installing; capabilities flip live on the existing stack.

Foundation

NetrIQ VMS

The video-management base every deployment starts on: multi-vendor ingestion (Hikvision, Dahua, CP Plus, Sparsh, ONVIF, RTSP), auto-discovery and bulk onboarding, live view, continuous recording, event-anchor playback, snapshot fallback, and baseline person/vehicle detection. Gate log with manual plate entry. AI add-ons activate per camera, no re-install.

Flagship

NetrIQ Suite

Everything in VMS plus the full AI surface: natural-language visual search, Persons-of-Interest with cross-camera re-identification, tailgating, PTZ & 360° dewarp, the zone-rules engine (unmanned-gate, occupancy, intrusion, tailgating), LPR with auto gate log & vehicle registry, multi-channel notify (in-app / email / WhatsApp), forensic export with chain-of-custody, themeable console, and multi-site role-based access.

Same engine, same camera support, same deployment options across both. Verticals — gated communities, hospitals, retail, hospitality, fleet, IT offices — are presets that run on either edition.

DEPLOYMENT

Cloud, on-prem, or both. Your data, your storage.

Cloud-managed

We host the console; the Site Agent at your premises bridges cameras over a relay. No port forwarding, no static IP, no VPN to maintain.

On-premise

Deploy the full stack on your own server. Hikvision, Dahua, ONVIF cameras feed in directly. Air-gapped if you want it that way.

Hybrid

Recording stays local for bandwidth and retention; detection metadata, alerts, and remote view ride the cloud. Default for most customers.

Edge
Linux server, Raspberry Pi 4 / 5, or x86 mini-PC. AP-mode setup for the Pi when there’s no DHCP yet.
Protocols
RTSP, ONVIF, RTMP (for SIM cams), WebRTC, HLS. H.264 and H.265.
Native vendors
Hikvision, Dahua / Amcrest, Frigate. Generic ONVIF for everything else.
Authentication
JWT, role-based access, per-site isolation, audit log of user actions.
Onboarding
Token-based site activation: admin generates a token, operator runs the installer, the site comes online by itself.
